STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

How VCTM Foundly Works

01

Report

Post a lost or found report with details, campus location, photos, and an optional secret verification question.

02

Smart Match

Our intelligent system matches category, title keywords, and campus locations, sending instant notifications.

03

Verify & Connect

Chat securely in-app, answer the verification challenge, and coordinate a safe exchange at official campus desks.

04

Reunite

Hand over the item at a designated campus safety point and mark the report as resolved in one click.
